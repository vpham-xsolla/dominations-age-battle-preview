Needs a decision before launch

  • Prize claim (ad rule R7): the end card promises a Crown item in the Xsolla Backpack and 1,000 Crowns. The reward must actually exist and be redeemable, or the text has to change.
  • Button label vs destination: “Redeem in Backpack” opens the App Store / Google Play, not a Backpack page. Change the label or supply the Backpack link.

Same as the Unity design

  • Layout of town center, towers, walls, splats and defenders, read straight from the Unity scene.
  • DomiNations art and per-unit idle / walk / attack animations; red defenders, blue attackers.
  • Three cards (Cavemen ×10, Knights ×5, Soldiers ×3), one unit per tap, BattleSim rules and tuning (player always wins).
  • End card title, prize text and green button from the project settings.

Different from the Unity design

  • Effects (fire, smoke, explosions, debris) are simpler hand-coded versions using the same textures.
  • Sound reuses DomiNations effects from the earlier ad — the Unity project has no audio. Mute button added.
  • Store links default to the DomiNations App Store / Play pages (none were set in Unity).
  • Portrait shows the whole battlefield; the Unity camera would cut off the town center.

Not yet checked

  • Real devices and ad-network preview tools (tested only in automated headless Chrome, both orientations, no errors).
  • Per-network packaging (Meta, Google, AppLovin, Unity, ironSource…).
